為什麼需要治理Why governance

公司的 AI，需要有人治理。 A company's AI needs someone to govern it.

多數公司的 AI 是由員工自行採用、逐步進入日常工作的。這種方式起步容易，卻缺少三樣東西：有人負責、有規則可循、有積累可留。 In most companies, AI arrived through employees adopting it on their own and folding it into everyday work. That is an easy way to start, but it leaves three things missing: someone responsible, rules to follow, and something that accumulates.

投入難以產生回報Investment struggles to pay back

麻省理工學院 2025 年的調研顯示，約 95% 的企業生成式 AI 試點未能產生可衡量的損益影響。差別不在於買了什麼工具，而在於是否有人負責把工具落到工作中。沒有治理，投入停留在採購。 MIT research in 2025 found that roughly 95% of enterprise generative-AI pilots produced no measurable impact on profit and loss. The difference lies not in which tools were bought, but in whether anyone was responsible for landing them in real work. Without governance, investment stops at procurement.

資料風險無人把關Data risk goes unattended

哪些資料可以交給 AI 處理，多數公司沒有成文的規則，全憑員工各自判斷。公司的資料邊界因此取決於個人的分寸；而在監管與客戶眼中，責任始終歸於公司。 Which data may be handed to an AI tool is, in most companies, not written down; employees judge for themselves. The company's data boundary therefore rests on individual discretion — while in the eyes of regulators and clients, the responsibility remains the company's.

能力無法持續積累Capability fails to accumulate

用得好的方法留在少數員工的習慣與個人帳號裡，公司看不見、留不住；人員流動，能力隨之離開。沒有治理，公司的 AI 能力始終停留在個人層面，不會隨時間累積。 The methods that work stay in the habits and personal accounts of a few employees; the company can neither see them nor keep them, and when people move on, the capability leaves with them. Without governance, a company's AI capability stays at the level of individuals and never compounds over time.

治理不是限制使用，而是讓投入有回報、風險有邊界、能力有積累。這需要一個持續運作的職能，而非一次採購。 Governance is not about restricting use. It is what makes investment pay back, keeps risk within bounds, and lets capability accumulate — and it takes a function that runs continuously, not a one-off purchase.

服務領域Services

三個階段，建起一個持續運作的職能。 Three stages. One function that keeps running.

01

諮詢Advisory

評估企業目前的 AI 使用狀況與風險，建立使用制度與資料邊界，規劃落地的優先次序。 Assess how the company currently uses AI and where the risks lie; establish usage policy and data boundaries; set priorities for implementation.

02

建設Build

搭建符合合規要求的 AI 工作環境，將有效的用法沉澱為公司自己的工具，並完成團隊導入。 Set up a compliant AI working environment, turn the practices that work into the company's own tools, and bring the team on board.

03

運營Operations

以固定節奏持續運行企業的 AI 職能：新用法的沉澱、資產與知識的維護、月度與季度使用報告、行業動向跟蹤。 Run the company's AI function on a fixed cadence: capturing new practice, maintaining assets and knowledge, monthly and quarterly usage reports, and tracking developments in the field.

我們的立場Our principles

持續經營，可隨時移交。 Long-term stewardship. Transferable at any time.

AI 相關的制度、工具與知識處於持續變化之中：模型更迭、用法演進、人員流動，任何一次性的交付都會在數月內失效。因此我們不以項目交付作為服務的終點，而是以長期經營的方式承擔企業的 AI 職能——承擔的是職能，決策始終在客戶。 AI-related policy, tools and knowledge are in constant motion: models change, practices evolve, people move on, and any one-off deliverable is out of date within months. We therefore do not treat project delivery as the end of the engagement; we take on the company's AI function as a matter of ongoing stewardship — the function is ours to carry; the decisions remain the client's.

長期合作必須建立在客戶隨時可以離開的基礎上。我們據此確立三項原則： A long-term engagement must rest on the client's freedom to leave. Three principles follow:

成果歸屬客戶Client ownership

合作期間形成的制度文件、工具與知識庫，均以客戶名義建立並登記，客戶對其擁有完整的權屬與使用權。 Policy documents, tools and knowledge bases created during the engagement are established and registered in the client's name, with full ownership and rights of use resting with the client.

資產可攜Portability

所有資產以結構化、可導出的形式建立與維護，並保持供應商中立，不與特定模型或平台綁定。客戶可隨時將其移交至內部團隊或其他服務方。 All assets are built and maintained in structured, exportable form and remain vendor-neutral, tied to no particular model or platform. They can be transferred to an in-house team or another provider at any time.

退出安排前置Exit defined at the outset

退出機制在合作開始時即已明確：客戶可依約定終止合作，並獲得完整的移交安排。持續合作的理由應當是價值，而非更換成本。 The terms of exit are settled when the engagement begins: the client may end the engagement as agreed and receives a complete handover. The reason to continue should be value, not the cost of switching.
